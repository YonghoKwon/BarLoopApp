## English (`en-US`)

- Name: `BarLoop: Drum Practice`
- Subtitle: `Loop trainer and metronome`
- Promotional text: `Loop the bar, lock in the click, and track every focused practice session.`
- Keywords: `drums,metronome,looper,BPM,rhythm,practice,MIDI,count-in,tempo,music`
- Description:

  `BarLoop is a local-first practice companion built for drummers.`

  `Import audio or video, or load a video with the official YouTube player. Loop a precise A–B range or generate bars from BPM and the first downbeat. Add pre-roll, count-in, Gap Click, and a progressive tempo trainer.`

  `The standalone metronome supports 20–400 BPM, 2–12 beats per bar, quarter, eighth, triplet, and sixteenth subdivisions, per-beat accents, swing, and multiple click sounds. A drum sequencer, moving-accent trainer, routines, MIDI controls, and practice history round out the toolkit.`

  `There is no account, advertising, or analytics SDK. Imported media and practice data stay on your device.`
